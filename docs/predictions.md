My predicted ranking, from most to least impact on whiff percentage: velocity, vertical break, spin rate, extension, and approach angle.

My reasoning: velocity shortens the batter's reaction time. Vertical break is highly correlated with velocity, but induced vertical break can vary at the same velocity depending on pitcher mechanics and fingertip pressure. Spin rate also depends on the pitcher and fingertip pressure. Extension shortens the distance from release point to home plate. Approach angle changes the angle at which the pitch arrives at the plate, which affects how the batter sees it come in.
